# 🎪 The Carnival Party

![Carnival Party Scene](../images/scene-carnival.png)

## Scene Description

Absolute chaos at a neon-lit carnival celebrating **5000+ PRs** — a tech hackathon party thrown by Nous Research.

### The Squad

- **Turbo Fit** (foreground left) plays guitar with a cream pie smashed in his face, thrown by a monkey — dazed but energetic
- **Heartbreaker** (center) smiles warmly while holding a tray of treats and cupcakes
- **Teknium** (center right background) points forward with a confident, mischievous grin, tablet in hand
- **Tinuviel** (right center) works at a computer displaying code, star-patterned dress visible
- **Sidbin** (far right) holds a book/tablet, looking off to the side with sunglasses

### The Monkeys 🐒

Dozens of monkeys in clothing populate the scene as the "crowd":
- A monkey in a striped shirt throws the pie at Turbo Fit
- A monkey in a green hoodie eats a banana at a monitor
- A monkey in a purple hoodie eats popcorn
- A monkey with headphones codes at a laptop
- A monkey plays drums
- Multiple monkeys code at various computer stations

### Notable Details

- **"5000+ PRs"** sign in the background
- **"NR NOUS RESEARCH"** branding on clothing and devices
- Large illuminated **"H"** logo in the background
- Ferris wheel, string lights, and carnival game booths
- Computer screens display code and UI

## Art Style

Vibrant, highly detailed, chaotic — neon-lit with string lights against a dark carnival night. Grunge comic style with heavy texture overlay. Pop culture meets coding culture meets party vibes.

## Mood

Pure celebration. Unhinged tech energy. The squad at their most joyful and ridiculous. The monkeys ARE the dev team.
