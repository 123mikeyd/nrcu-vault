> **Community member — confirmed appearance**

## Confirmed identity and appearance

The archive owner identifies **Witcheer** as the anthropomorphic duck communicating with **Kainan** in [*Thank You Nous Research* by Arts Bro](../music-videos/thank-you-nous-research-arts-bro.md). Witcheer is a community member; Kainan appears on the screen as a simple black and white logo.

The appearance is at the one minute mark.

## Character-sheet details

The supplied reference sheet states:

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Height** | 4'2" (127 cm) |
| **Build** | Compact / Sharp |
| **Role** | Luck Merchant |
| **Weapon** | Fortune Cane |
| **Allegiance** | House of Gold |

The sheet describes Witcheer as a slick, cunning, charismatic trickster who turns luck into leverage and wealth into power. It says the **Fortune Cane** channels probability and opportunity, allowing a tap to twist fate, tilt odds, or open doors—for a price.

## Visible design anchors

- Cream-white feathers and a saturated-orange bill
- Large obsidian-black shades
- Long crimson coat with polished-gold trim
- Crowned **W** emblem on the medallion, coat back, and cane
- Black cane with gold fittings
- Patent-black footwear with gold detailing
- Floating coin motif and ink-and-gold splatter accents
