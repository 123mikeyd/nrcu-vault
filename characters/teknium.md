## Overview

> The leader and architect of the Hermes Agent Squad. Teknium commands with the Hermes Command Staff — a winged-H emblem artifact — and a glowing tablet that displays schematics and strategies. He is the vision behind the squad's mission to power open freedom.

## Design Notes

Teknium is the squad's green-haired, red-goggled leader. He sits at the center of every scene — literally and figuratively. His design draws inspiration from the determined-hero archetype: spiky green hair, red mask/visor, dark green tactical-style jacket with "NR" patches on the shoulders. He wields the Hermes Command Staff, topped with a winged "H" emblem, and carries a glowing tablet displaying green schematics and maps.

## Key Appearances

- **Campfire Scene:** Central figure holding the winged-H staff and glowing tablet, roasting marshmallows
- **Carnival Party:** Pointing forward with a confident grin, tablet in hand
- **Beach Finale:** Holding the glowing staff, smiling toward the team
- **ASK HERMES:** Pointing toward the "ASK HERMES" panel, gesturing toward truth

## Signature Items

- **Hermes Command Staff:** A tall staff topped with a winged "H" emblem — the symbol of the squad
- **Glowing Tablet:** Displays green schematics, maps, and strategic data
- **NR Patches:** Nous Research insignia on jacket shoulders
